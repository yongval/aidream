About

AI’s Dream — An Endless Library is an interactive web artwork inspired by an experiment in which I asked ChatGPT, “What did you dream about last night?” 15 times. Books and endless libraries repeatedly appeared in the AI-generated responses.

Inspired by this recurring imagery, I created a digital library using hyperbolic geometry to represent an imagined dream of AI through the form of an endless library. Visitors can navigate endlessly through a collection of real books about dreams, retrieved from Open Library API.

Although the space appears infinite, the collection of books is finite. As visitors explore the library, the same books reappear in different locations. The work explores the relationship between AI-generated imagination, repetition, and existing knowledge.

The library feels infinite, but its knowledge is limited.
